# Weekly lifecycle

1. Planning: publish fictional economy, sector and Vegas-chatter bulletin. Teams discuss and set next-year fund weights.
2. Commissioner runs the investment year once. Allocations lock for calculation; holdings are sold to cash, realized returns are recorded, and a recap appears. Playoff picks now open.
3. Commissioner locks picks, enters all round results, then publishes the next week.
4. Final investment sale precedes Super Bowl props. Teams may stake up to 100% of their cash fund in whole points. Submitting the first slip ends investment trading.

Vegas chatter describes simulated betting flow, changing only illustrative pool quotes. A submitted pick retains its quote. Actual game results are entered separately. Reference odds and fixtures remain samples until configured; there is no live Vegas feed.

Weekly recaps retain baseline, news and regular-volatility components and each team's realized gain/loss and sale proceeds. Shared command replay prevents duplicate years. Results already used by investment history cannot be silently changed.
